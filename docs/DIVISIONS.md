# Product Divisions

Divisions are product capability boundaries. They are not necessarily separate teams or repositories.

1. **Websites & Experience Division** — marketing sites, landing pages, business sites, portfolios, content-driven experiences.
2. **Applications & SaaS Division** — dashboards, admin portals, SaaS foundations, authenticated applications.
3. **Commerce Division** — storefronts, catalogs, checkout-ready UI foundations, inventory and order operations (payment integrations require separate approval and compliance review).
4. **Design Systems Division** — tokens, UI kits, component libraries, responsive patterns, accessibility.
5. **Documents & Resume Division** — CVs, resumes, proposals, reports, business documents, document layouts.
6. **Automation & Workflow Division** — reusable workflow definitions, operational checklists, integration blueprints.
7. **Developer Platform & DevOps Division** — repo scaffolds, CI/CD examples, observability, deployment templates, developer tooling.
8. **AI & Data Solutions Division** — AI-enabled application patterns, retrieval/knowledge patterns, data dashboards, evaluation scaffolds.
9. **Sector Solutions Division** — cross-division bundles tailored to an industry.
10. **Platform & Governance Division** — catalog, authoring, versioning, generation, access control, audit, release governance.

Each division should eventually have a charter, owner, scope, supported artifact types, standards, roadmap, and acceptance checklist.
