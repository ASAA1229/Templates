# Governance and Safety

- Keep the repository private unless public release is explicitly approved.
- Never commit credentials, tokens, private client data, personal records, or production exports.
- Record ownership and licensing for every imported asset and template.
- Do not redistribute third-party assets without clear rights.
- Require human approval before publishing, selling, licensing, creating client repositories, or deploying generated projects.
- Separate internal experiments, approved reusable assets, and client-specific deliverables.
- Apply least-privilege access and audit important administrative/generation actions.
- Treat healthcare, finance, legal, and other regulated sector templates as requiring domain-expert and jurisdiction-specific review.
- Generated client work must be isolated; do not leak one client's content, configuration, secrets, or assets into another project.
- AI integrations must be optional, transparent, scoped, and subject to explicit user approval for consequential actions.
