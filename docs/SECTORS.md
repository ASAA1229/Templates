# Sector Directory (Planned)

This is a taxonomy and set of placeholders, not a claim that sector-specific templates exist.

## Initial sector groups
- General Business & Professional Services
- Food, Beverage, Restaurants & Hospitality
- Retail, Wholesale & E-commerce
- Healthcare, Wellness & Clinics
- Education, Training & Learning
- Real Estate, Construction & Property
- Finance, Accounting & Insurance (informational/business tooling only; regulated workflows require specialist review)
- Technology, IT & SaaS
- Manufacturing, Industrial & Logistics
- Travel, Tourism & Events
- Media, Entertainment & Creative
- Non-profit, Community & Public-interest
- Personal Brands, Freelancers & Consultants
- Enterprise & Internal Operations

## Sector pack placeholder standard
Each future sector pack should define: overview, target users, common workflows, required pages/modules, optional modules, data sensitivity, accessibility needs, integrations, compliance considerations, sample content, acceptance criteria, and support owner.

## Status
All entries above are proposed classification areas. No sector pack is marked implemented.
