# Taxonomy and Repository Organization

## Top-level template families
- Websites & Web Experiences
- Applications & SaaS
- Commerce & Retail
- UI Kits & Design Systems
- Resumes, CVs & Professional Documents
- Business Operations & Automation
- Developer & DevOps Tooling
- Data, AI & Knowledge Solutions
- Education & Learning
- Creative & Media Assets
- Industry/Sector Solution Packs
- Other Digital Products

## Classification dimensions
Every catalog item should support:
- Product family and subcategory
- Sector(s) and business function(s)
- Division/maintainer
- Artifact type (source, starter, component, document, workflow, design asset)
- Technology/framework and version constraints
- Target audience and complexity
- License/usage rights and redistribution restrictions
- Maturity state (idea, placeholder, draft, alpha, beta, stable, deprecated, archived)
- Accessibility, security, and quality status
- Owner, version, changelog, dependencies, and compatibility

## Naming
Use stable kebab-case identifiers. Names are labels, not authorization. Classification must not imply that a template is complete, tested, licensed, or approved for client use.
