# Product Vision

## Mission
Provide one organized, dependable workspace for reusable digital assets and repeatable delivery workflows across ASAA projects.

## Product position
Templates Studio is a standalone product/repository. It is not a subfolder or feature of the public Portfolio. Portfolio may display selected, approved examples. JARVIS may be an optional AI collaborator through a secure integration boundary.

## Intended audiences
- Initially private/internal use by the ASAA owner.
- Future optional team collaboration.
- Public or commercial distribution only after licensing, permissions, security, and product-readiness decisions.

## Product capabilities (planned)
1. Template catalog, taxonomy, search, tags, and lifecycle state.
2. Template authoring/import, preview, validation, and version history.
3. Configurable customization profiles for brand, content, layout, features, integrations, and deployment targets.
4. Independent project generation/export, with optional repository creation.
5. Sector packs and division-specific workflows.
6. Resume/document studio and design-system library.
7. Optional AI assistance, including a future JARVIS connector.
8. Future licensing, client handoff, and marketplace capabilities.

## Non-goals for initial foundation
- No production SaaS implementation in this initialization.
- No public marketplace or payments.
- No automatic deployment to client environments.
- No forced dependency on JARVIS, Portfolio, or a single technology stack.
- No assumption that every proposed category is approved for release.

## Product principles
Private-first; modular; framework-adaptive; reusable but not restrictive; client isolation; secure by default; accessible; observable; documented; free-first; human-controlled.
