# Product Vision

## Mission
Provide one secure, owner-controlled workspace to build, test, deploy, monitor, maintain and deliver digital products efficiently through reusable engineering assets, standardized workflows, automation and optional AI assistance.

## Product position
ASAA Templates is initially a **private internal software-development and business-operations platform**, not a public template marketplace. It supports the owner's work and future company operations. The initial user is the owner alone; staff and employees are future users, not assumed to exist today.

In the future, the platform may support multiple isolated organizations and may be offered to other companies as a product or service.

## Business outcomes
- Reduce repeated engineering effort, delivery time and project cost.
- Improve consistency, security, test coverage and delivery quality.
- Reuse templates, components, design systems, workflows and integrations.
- Increase delivery capacity and sustainable profitability.
- Provide a path from software engineering operations to broader business operations.

## Operating constraints
- ₹0 operating spend until the owner explicitly authorizes spending.
- Prefer local-first, free/open-source and provider-independent options.
- No paid APIs, AI models, hosted services, subscriptions, infrastructure or licenses without explicit approval.
- Owner retains ultimate platform authority; delegated permissions are explicit and auditable.

## Initial audiences
- Initial: owner only.
- Later internal: administrators, employees, developers, designers, teams and departments.
- Future external: independent organizations with isolated identity, data, configuration, projects and administration.

## Planned capabilities
1. Owner workspace, dashboard, identity and access management.
2. Project, requirements, task and delivery management.
3. Private catalog and reusable template/component/asset management.
4. Project initialization from template, composition, imported project or scratch.
5. Universal technology, repository and integration adapters.
6. Development workspace, build, testing, quality gates and documentation.
7. CI/CD, deployment, observability, incident and recovery workflows.
8. Optional AI assistance and selected JARVIS capabilities through a dedicated integration boundary.
9. Future CRM, sales, proposals, quotations, contracts, finance, HR, support, procurement, analytics, legal/compliance and business automation modules.
10. Future multi-organization and productization capability, subject to security and product-readiness validation.

## JARVIS boundary
JARVIS remains a separate product. Approved capabilities may include chatbot, specialized agents, monitoring bot, model orchestration and reusable AI modules. Personal JARVIS memory, private device control and personal capabilities must never be exposed to company users. Company agents have separate identities, memory, tools, permissions, audit and approval policies.

## Non-goals for initial MVP
- Public marketplace or payments.
- Paid cloud/AI/service dependency.
- Full enterprise SaaS tenancy before the internal MVP is validated.
- Full accounting/payroll/HR or every future business module.
- Premature microservices or complex distributed infrastructure.
- Automatic publication, client deployment or high-risk AI actions without approval.

## Product principles
Private-first; owner-controlled; ₹0 until authorized; modular; framework-adaptive; provider-independent; secure by default; tenant-aware by design; accessible; observable; documented; independently generated client outputs; human approval for consequential actions.
